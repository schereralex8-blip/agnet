MATH 152 — Problem Set 3
Due Friday. Show your work for full credit.

1. Differentiate f(x) = x^3 + 2x - 7.

2. Find the derivative of y = x^x. (Hint: the power rule does not apply here.
   Neither does the exponential rule. Think about what to do to both sides first.)

3. Evaluate the integral of x*e^x dx.

4. A ball is dropped from rest at a height of 20 m. Taking g = 9.81 m/s^2 and
   ignoring air resistance, how long does it take to hit the ground? Give your
   answer to three significant figures.

5. A fair six-sided die is rolled four times. What is the probability of getting
   at least one 6? Give the answer as an exact fraction and as a decimal.

6. State the Intermediate Value Theorem, and use it to show that
   x^3 - x - 1 = 0 has a root between 1 and 2.
